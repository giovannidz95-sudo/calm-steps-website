More than a walk

For the dogs who need a little more.

Some dogs settle into any group and any routine without a second thought. Others find the outside world too loud, too fast and too busy — they pull, freeze, bark or hold back, and a crowded walk only makes it harder.

Calm Steps is made for those dogs. Walks are unhurried and predictable: quiet routes, gentle handling and a steady rhythm that gives a nervous dog the space to relax rather than stay on edge.

The aim is simple — a dog that comes home properly tired, a little calmer, and slowly more at ease outdoors.

“Calm walking, done properly.”

The person behind the lead

Hi, I'm Giovanni.

Calm Steps grew from years spent walking with my own dog, Whiskey.

Through our daily walks, I learned how much difference a calm, attentive approach can make. Some dogs need excitement and movement. Others need space, patience and a steady presence to feel comfortable outdoors.

“A good walk is not about distance. It is about attention, trust and rhythm.”

Whiskey is calm and well trained today, and he often helps create a more relaxed atmosphere around other dogs. That experience shaped the way I walk: not rushed, not crowded, and always focused on the dog in front of me.

I keep Calm Steps intentionally small, so I can understand each dog's personality, adapt to their rhythm and make every walk feel safe, calm and enjoyable.
